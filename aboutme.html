ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Hasan Jaffal

I work at the intersection of AI, people, and business outcomes. This site is where I examine the opportunities, decisions, and evidence behind AI adoption.

PROFESSIONAL MISSION

What becomes possible—and what changes as a result?

My mission is to help business leaders find the opportunities AI makes possible and lead the transformation it takes to realize them. I start with a question: what would we change for our customers or our business if we could do something today that was out of reach yesterday?

I work with the people who know the business best to test the answer and build the skills to put it into practice. Before investing, we agree on the business outcome and how we will measure it. We then assess how much of the improvement we can attribute to AI and whether it justified the investment.

EXPERIENCE

Analytics, data, and product leadership.

My background spans software engineering, product and technology leadership, and analytics in operational environments. My work at Amazon has included security and loss prevention, building an international data science organization, and taking loss forecasting and alerting use cases from proof of value to deployment across regions.

Earlier roles included leading a project management office at ArabiaGIS and product, engineering, data, and content teams at Nabed. Across these settings, the recurring questions have been which problems deserve attention, what people need to change, and how to judge the result.

THE STANDARD BEHIND THE WRITING

A decision you can make more confidently.

I want this site to be useful to leaders deciding where AI could make a difference in their own organizations. I examine the opportunity, the alternatives, the people doing the work, and the connection between the change and the outcome. Where the evidence leaves uncertainty, that uncertainty belongs in the discussion.

The library includes articles and frameworks. An illustrative example explains a decision; a documented business case needs evidence about what happened. I aim to keep that distinction clear as the site develops.

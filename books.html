THE BOOK

The Second Mind

Can Learning Machines Help Us Stay Human?

An exploration of how artificial intelligence is reshaping the way we think, create, and lead—and what it means to stay human as we learn to work with it.

The book traces the evolution of intelligence from fire and language to algorithms and neural networks. It explores shared intelligence through decision-making, creativity, leadership, and ethics, drawing on stories and personal experience.
